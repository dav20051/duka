Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Sales are saved on the phone and sent automatically when the network comes back. You need internet once to log in and set up.

What phone do I need?

Any smartphone with a browser: Android or iPhone. It also works on a computer. No app store download needed, though you can add it to your home screen.

Is my data safe?

Each shop's records are kept separate and protected at the database level. Staff accounts can't see profit or change prices unless you allow it. You can download your data any time.

How do I pay?

By M-Pesa. After your free 30 days, choose a plan in the app and pay. If you stop paying, your records stay safe and read-only until you renew.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Add them by email. You choose what each person may do: sell on credit, void sales, record expenses or give discounts.

Is it in Kiswahili?

Yes. Switch between Kiswahili and English at any time.
