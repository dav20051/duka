Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Duka ("we", "us") provides shop management software for sales, stock and customer debts. This policy explains what personal data we handle and how, in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Who is responsible

[Business name], [physical address], Kenya. Contact: [email] · [phone / WhatsApp].

For the account you create, we are the data controller. For the records a shop keeps about its own customers (names, phone numbers, balances), the shop is the data controller and we act as its data processor.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and an encrypted password.
  • Shop data: shop name, phone, type, staff email addresses, products, prices, stock, sales, expenses and M-Pesa transaction codes you enter.
  • Your customers' data: names, phone numbers and amounts owed, entered by the shop.
  • Subscription data: payment dates, amounts and M-Pesa codes for your Duka subscription.
  • Technical data: basic logs (IP address, browser, time of request) kept by our hosting providers for security.

Why we use it

  • To run the service you signed up for (contract).
  • To keep accounts secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interest).
  • To manage your subscription and contact you about your account (contract).
  • To meet legal and tax obligations (legal obligation).

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run Duka: Supabase (database and login) and Vercel (website hosting). Their servers may be outside Kenya; we rely on their contractual and security safeguards for these transfers. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep shop records while your account is active. If your subscription ends, your data stays read-only so you can download it. You can ask us to delete your shop and its data at any time; we delete it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted per shop at the database level, and staff accounts cannot see profit or cost information.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, download or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Shop owners can download their sales, stock and customer records from the app at any time. Contact us at [email]. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

Shops' responsibilities

If you record your customers' details, you must have a lawful reason to do so and tell them how you use their information, for example for debt reminders.

Changes

We will post updates here and tell account holders about important changes.
